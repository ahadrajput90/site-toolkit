      Guides
      
          
      Short reads for better project decisions.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
            What actually drives the cost of a website?
            
      Page count matters less than people expect. The big levers are how much design is bespoke, how many integrations are involved (payments, bookings, email, languages), and how ready the content is. A five-page site with finished copy and photography is often cheaper to build than a three-page site where everything is still being written.
      
          
      
          
      
            Five things to settle before you ask for a quote
            
      Know your single main goal, list the pages you expect, decide who supplies the content, confirm who owns the domain and hosting, and name one person who can approve work. Missing any of these is the most common source of delays and extra charges.
      
          
      
          
      
            How to read a freelancer's estimate
            
      Look for what is included and excluded: revisions, content entry, migration, training, and post-launch support. Two quotes with the same total can cover very different work. Ask for the assumptions behind the number, then compare those.
      
          
      
          
      
            What belongs in a good project brief
            
      Goal, audience, scope, deliverables, timeline, budget range, references, constraints and a decision-maker. It does not need to be long. One clear page beats five vague ones.
